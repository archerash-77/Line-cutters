LINE CUTTERS

Privacy Policy — Last updated: June 2025

Line Cutters is a free archery scoring app for AGB rounds. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data We Collect

Line Cutters does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no login, and no connection to any server.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device only using your browser's localStorage:

  • Your archer profile (name, gender, age category)
  • Round history and scores
  • Equipment setups and sight marks
  • App preferences and favourites

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to any server, and we have no access to it.

Third Party Services

Line Cutters uses the following third party services to deliver the app:

  • Vercel — hosts the app. Vercel may log standard web server data such as IP addresses and browser type for security purposes. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — loads the Roboto and Bebas Neue fonts. Google may log font requests. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Line Cutters does not knowingly collect any data from children or any other users. No personal information is collected or stored on our servers.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
